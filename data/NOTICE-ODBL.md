# The historical-clock outlines: data notice

This notice applies to `mean_time_outlines.json`, beside it: the outlines of the areas the app reads by
coordinates for historical clocks (areas that kept their own time, cities whose public clocks took a zone's time on
dates of their own, and areas a zone covers today that were not then part of the country whose clocks its notes
describe), with the fields that go with them. The file is the complete collection, in machine-readable form.

The outlines have been modified for Zīj. They include selected municipal, departmental and provincial outlines,
simplification, replacement edges, small boxes and hand-drawn additions; the file says how each outline was drawn
and names the OpenStreetMap objects it was taken from where those are known. These are modified application data,
drawn to place a birthplace inside or outside an area, not an authoritative record of present or historical
borders.

saved charts) came from OpenStreetMap.
